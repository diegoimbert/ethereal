This page explains the **share** button and helps you pick the right kind of sharing before you send a link to anyone. You need an open project: the button is greyed out on the welcome screen.

## two ways to share

Click **share** in the top bar. The popover has two tabs:

| | **live collaboration** | **static snapshot** |
|---|---|---|
| what people get | the project itself, live: they edit or listen while you work | a rendered, read-only copy of the project as it is now |
| do you need to be online? | yes, your Ethereal must be open for anyone to join | no, the link works while you're offline |
| does it follow your changes? | yes, every edit syncs | no, it's frozen until you publish again |
| good for | working on a song together, getting feedback while you mix | sending a demo, posting a song, letting people open their own copy |

Live sessions are explained in [live sessions](/docs/sharing/live-sessions), and snapshots in [static share links](/docs/sharing/static-links).

## no account needed

Nobody needs an account, neither you nor the people you invite. Someone joining a live session picks a display name and a colour (**you appear as**). These are stored only on their own device, and they can change them in the share popover or in **settings › sharing**. Opening a static link needs nothing at all: it plays in any browser, phones included.

## can edit and can listen

A live session has two separate links:

- **can edit**: the person changes the project with you.
- **can listen**: the person hears your mix, sees the project, follows along, can solo and mute tracks and can chat, but can't edit anything. They get a **view only** badge.

The tabs above the link in the share popover choose which link the **copy** button copies. Check the tab before you copy, so you don't hand out edit rights by mistake.

A static link has its own permissions instead (listening to tracks separately, download, opening a copy to edit): see [static share links](/docs/sharing/static-links#permissions).

## anyone with the link gets in

A link works like a key: whoever has it can use it, with the role it gives. When you copy a live link, the toast says so: "anyone with this link can edit" (or "can listen").

> [!warning]
> If you post an edit link in a group chat, everyone in that chat can edit your project. To shut a link out, reset it: see [reset, remove or stop](/docs/sharing/live-sessions#reset-remove-or-stop).
