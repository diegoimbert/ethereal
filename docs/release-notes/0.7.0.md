> **If you installed v0.6.0 on desktop, reinstall once from https://ethereal.ws/download.** v0.6.0 can't find this update by itself, because its updater reads a repository that no longer exists. From this version on, updates install from the app again.

### Your data moves with the app
- The desktop app now identifies itself as `ws.ethereal.daw`. On first launch, your settings, plugin database, caches, autosaves and logs are moved to the new location. Nothing is deleted, and projects stay in `Documents/Ethereal/Projects`.
- **macOS asks again for microphone and screen recording access** the first time they're needed, because macOS ties these permissions to the app's identifier. Allow them once more.

### Devices
- **Select several devices** with Cmd/Ctrl-click and Shift-click, MIDI effects, instruments and audio effects together, or all of a chain with Cmd/Ctrl+A. Copy, cut, paste, delete and Cmd/Ctrl+G (group into a rack) act on the whole selection.
- A paste puts each device in its own section; pasting an instrument where there is one asks before replacing it. With no device selected, a paste goes to the end of the selected track's chain.
- **Drag a device from the add menu** straight into the chain, at the place you want.
- **Drag devices onto a track in the arrangement** to move them there (hold Cmd/Ctrl to copy).
- **Replace device** is a popover like the add menu: pick the replacement and it's done, with "retain automations and parameters" at the top.
- A device in a rack chain can **sidechain another chain of the same rack**.
- **Each plugin is listed once**, in your preferred format (CLAP first). Turn it off, change the preferred format, or hide plugins, in Settings › Plugins › Plugin list.
- **Freeze up to a device**: freeze a track's chain up to its instrument (or any device) and keep the effects after it live.
- Stopping playback no longer cuts reverb and delay tails, and a sidechained compressor releases instead of clicking.

### Arrangement and editing
- Track headers show mute, solo, arm and automation at all times, with bigger buttons, value readouts on the fader and pan knob, and a compact layout when tracks are small.
- Right-click any value (a device parameter, volume, pan, a send, clip gain) to set an exact value, reset it or show its automation.
- The selection box scrolls the view when dragged past its edges, in the arrangement and the piano roll.
- Smoother vertical zoom, bigger fade handles, and a colour palette on a double-click of a track's icon.
- Clicking a clip no longer opens panels; a double-click opens the editor.

### Interface
- **Settings in categories**, each with its pages, and no more jumping while a page loads.
- The **status bar** holds what was floating or crowding the top bar: missing plugins and samples, imports, safe mode, uploads, and the settings button.
- The top bar gets compact on smaller screens; tap tempo moved to the metronome's right-click menu.
- Every control shows a tooltip on hover.
- The project menu opens recent projects and the tutorials as submenus.
- Library file rules in plain words (file type, name starts with…), with regular expressions for advanced use.
- No more macOS autocorrect in search fields.
- No glow in the light theme.

### Audio and plugins
- Audio device fixes on Linux (the useful ALSA devices, a 512-frame default buffer) and more reliable Audio Unit plugins.
