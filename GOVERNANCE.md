# Governance

Ethereal is a solo-maintainer project: [@diegoimbert](https://github.com/diegoimbert) (see
[MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md)) leads it and has the final say.

## Decisions

- **Day-to-day changes** are decided in issues and pull requests. Anyone can propose a change;
  the maintainer reviews it and merges it or explains why not.
- **Larger changes** (new features, file-format or protocol changes, dependencies with a wide
  footprint) start as an issue, so the approach is agreed before the work
  ([CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#before-you-start)).
- **Direction** is set by the maintainer and recorded in [docs/ROADMAP.md](docs/ROADMAP.md).

## Releases

The maintainer decides when a version is released and publishes it: the official installers,
the web app at [ethereal.ws](https://ethereal.ws) and the release notes. The process is in
[docs/RELEASING.md](docs/RELEASING.md); every release is listed in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

## Licensing

Ethereal is licensed under AGPL-3.0-or-later. Contributors sign the [CLA](CLA.md), which lets
the copyright holder (Diego Imbert) also offer Ethereal under other terms (dual licensing) and
relicense it in the future. Licensing decisions are the copyright holder's alone; see
[LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md).

## Conduct

Everyone in the project's spaces follows the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md), which the
maintainer enforces.

## Becoming a maintainer

The maintainer may invite regular contributors with a record of good work to review and merge
in an area. New maintainers are added to [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md) and
[`.github/CODEOWNERS`](.github/CODEOWNERS). This document will change if the project grows.
